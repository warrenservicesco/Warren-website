HOW AN ENGAGEMENT WORKS

Clear objectives. Defined scope. Useful recommendations.

Understand the need

Establish the problem, desired outcome, relevant context, and what a useful result would look like.

Agree on the scope

Define the work to be undertaken, the information required, expected deliverables, timing, and responsibilities.

Conduct the analysis

Examine the available evidence, identify constraints, and evaluate relevant explanations or alternatives.

Present the findings

Explain the conclusions, supporting reasoning, limitations, and potential next steps in an agreed format.

The actual process and deliverables will depend on the agreed scope of each engagement.
