EDITORIAL STANDARD

Useful information, carefully presented.

WARREN's planned editorial approach emphasizes clarity, relevant evidence, transparent reasoning, and a practical connection between the problem being discussed and the conclusions being presented.

Published articles should be reviewed for factual accuracy, appropriate citations, scope, readability, and the distinction between established facts, interpretation, and opinion.

Examples should be identified accurately. Hypothetical scenarios must not be presented as real client engagements or verified business results.
